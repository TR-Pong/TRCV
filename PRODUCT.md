# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Primary users are recruiters and hiring teams reviewing a candidate's background, technical strengths, project work, and fit for software or game development roles. Secondary users include clients, collaborators, and technical peers who want a quick but credible view of experience, skills, and shipped work.

## Product Purpose

Spatial Curiosity is a bilingual CV and portfolio site for presenting professional experience, technical capability, and selected software or game projects. Success means a visitor can quickly understand who Tanakhom is, what kind of work he does, what he has built, and how to contact him for hiring or collaboration.

## Brand Personality

Professional, reliable, technical. The experience should feel focused, capable, and quietly confident, with enough craft to signal care without distracting from the CV content and project evidence.

## Anti-references

This should not look like a noisy template portfolio, a flashy effects demo, or a decorative landing page that hides the actual work. Avoid cluttered card grids, exaggerated motion, vague marketing copy, and visuals that feel more like a startup pitch than a candidate profile.

## Design Principles

- Lead with credibility: make role, experience, projects, and contact routes easy to scan.
- Let the work carry the story: project images, descriptions, and tech stacks should be clear before decorative styling.
- Keep bilingual reading comfortable: Thai and English should both feel intentional, readable, and professional.
- Favor calm structure over spectacle: visual polish should support trust, not compete with content.
- Make admin updates practical: the backoffice should stay compact, predictable, and efficient for repeated editing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA for contrast, keyboard access, focus states, and readable typography. Support reduced motion where animation is used. Preserve clear Thai and English text rendering, avoiding wide tracking or forced uppercase treatments that make Thai harder to read.
